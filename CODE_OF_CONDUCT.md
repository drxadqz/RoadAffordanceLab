# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We aim to keep collaboration respectful, evidence-based and welcoming. Constructive disagreement about methods and results is encouraged; personal attacks, harassment and discriminatory behavior are not.

Examples of positive behavior include:

- separating measured evidence from interpretation;
- reporting limitations and negative results honestly;
- giving reproducible technical feedback;
- respecting dataset, model and software licenses;
- protecting private data and credentials.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior is abusive, unsafe or intentionally misleading. Report serious concerns through the private channel described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
